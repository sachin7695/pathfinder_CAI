Section: ICDS Guidelines — Nutrition
Supplementary Nutrition for Children

All children aged 6 months to 6 years should receive supplementary nutrition at the Anganwadi Centre daily.

Infants (0–6 months): Only breast milk. No water, honey, or animal milk should be given.

Children 6–24 months: In addition to breast milk, complementary foods such as khichdi, mashed fruits, and porridge must be introduced.

Pregnant and lactating women should receive one hot cooked meal daily at the Anganwadi Centre.

Meal Frequency Recommendations

6–8 months: 2–3 meals/day + breast milk.

9–11 months: 3–4 meals/day + 1–2 snacks + breast milk.

12–24 months: 3–4 meals/day + 1–2 snacks + breast milk.

Hygiene and Feeding

Always wash hands before preparing or serving food.

Use clean bowls and spoons; do not feed children with hands.

Discard uneaten food after 2 hours.

Section: Growth Monitoring & Child Health
Weighing and Measuring

Every child at the centre must be weighed monthly and their height measured every quarter.

Use the digital weighing scale and infantometer/stadiometer as per instructions.

Record the weight and height in the Poshan Tracker or Growth Monitoring Register.

Look for rapid drops in weight—refer such children to the ANM or PHC immediately.

Child Growth Chart Color Codes

Green: Normal weight for age

Yellow: Moderate underweight—counsel parents and monitor closely.

Red: Severe underweight—refer to nearest health facility.

Immunization

Ensure children receive vaccines as per the government schedule.

Remind mothers to bring the child’s Mother-Child Protection Card at every visit.

Section: Poshan Tracker — Data Entry & Reporting
Steps for Recording a Child’s Data

Open the Poshan Tracker app.

Select “Add New Beneficiary.”

Enter the child’s name, age (in completed months), gender, and Aadhaar (if available).

Enter monthly weight (kg) and height (cm).

Choose the correct nutritional status (normal, moderate, severe).

Save and sync data when online.

Common Data Entry Errors

Entering weight in grams instead of kilograms.

Skipping height entry.

Selecting the wrong nutritional status.

Failing to sync data—always check for “data uploaded successfully” message.

Section: Sanitation & Early Child Development
Hygiene Practices

Wash hands with soap after using the toilet and before feeding children.

Clean the Anganwadi Centre daily.

Use covered containers for drinking water.

Early Childhood Activities

Organize play sessions with age-appropriate toys.

Tell stories, sing songs, and encourage children to talk and express themselves.

Section: Anganwadi Worker Responsibilities
Daily Tasks

Prepare and serve hot cooked meals.

Take attendance of children and pregnant/lactating women.

Record distribution of Take-Home Rations (THR).

Counsel mothers on nutrition, hygiene, and child care.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What to do if a child misses several days at the centre?
A: Visit the home, counsel the parents, and encourage regular attendance. Note the reason for absence in the register.

Q: What should be done in case of a malnourished child?
A: Refer immediately to the nearest health facility or ANM. Counsel the family and monitor the child’s progress weekly.
